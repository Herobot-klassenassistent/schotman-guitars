The Luthier

Robert Schotman

After eight years refining his craft alongside Henri Sattler at Serpent King Guitars, Robert launched his own name with one rule: the highest possible quality, nothing outsourced.

Every guitar is built from raw wood to finish in his own workshop — modern CNC precision married to years of hands-on luthier experience. The result is instruments that are custom, unique, and mostly one-off.

The signature 8141 line is named after his hometown's postal code — endless small details that celebrate Heino and the Salland region, and the friends and family behind the brand.

About us

A small workshop with
an outsized obsession.

Schotman Guitars was founded in 2021 in Heino, in the Dutch Salland region. It is Robert — and the friends and family who back him — designing, building and finishing every instrument by hand, in-house, from raw wood to the final setup. No production line. No outsourcing. Mostly one-offs.

01

Everything in-house

Design, CNC, carving, fretwork, custom paint, electronics and setup — all done under one roof by one luthier. The buck stops with Robert.

02

One of one

Exclusive designs and hand-picked wood mean most Schotmans are unique. Your guitar is built around you, not a catalogue.

03

Local & personal

Built with local people and trusted partners — Schaller, Floyd Rose, Kahler, Railhammer. The 8141 line even carries Heino's postal code.
